Research · ICLR 2026 workshop

Script Kiddie Uplift

We study how mid-2025 LLM-generated code uplifts novice attackers, and whether models assist harmful goals when requests are decomposed into benign substeps.

Venue
ICLR 2026, Agents in the Wild workshop
Authors
Che, Poveda, Rodriguez, He, Zhang, Wang, Sehwag
My role
Research operations, data gathering, quality assurance
  • Step-by-step interactions rarely trigger refusal, even when the same models recognize safety concerns in separate evaluation.
  • Code-assisted novices progressed nearly one additional step further on average than an internet-only baseline.
  • Weaker performers benefited disproportionately, closing on average 47% of the gap to top performers.

Read the paper on OpenReview

About

About me

I'm Aldana, Argentine born and raised but now based in Berlin. I'm the head of the Agentic Pod at LinkedIn AI, where I lead data programs for reinforcement-learning environments and agentic workflows. Before that I ran human-data programs for frontier AI labs at Scale AI, including a cybersecurity research project with more than 50 PhD candidates.

What I care about most is reducing (and ultimately ending) suffering of all sentient creatures. This includes animal welfare, but also reducing the risks that come with AGI and ASI.

I've taken the Giving What We Can 10% Pledge: every month I donate 10% of my salary through Effektiv Spenden.
